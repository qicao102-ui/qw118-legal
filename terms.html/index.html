The service

QW118 is a desktop content tool for brands and creators. You authorize a TikTok account in the tool, choose a finished video on that desktop, and QW118 uploads the video to that account’s TikTok drafts (inbox). The person who uses the authorized account can then open the draft in the TikTok app, review it, and publish it there if they decide to.

QW118 does not post, schedule, or publish videos automatically. Sending a file through QW118 creates a draft in the authorized account. It does not put that video on a TikTok profile, and it does not choose a publish time. Publishing, if it happens at all, is a separate step that someone takes inside the TikTok app.
