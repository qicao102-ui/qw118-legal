Privacy Policy

QW118 is a desktop content tool that uploads finished videos to TikTok drafts. The creator reviews and publishes those drafts in the TikTok app. QW118 does not publish videos automatically.

Data we use: TikTok OAuth tokens and the video files you choose to upload.

Purpose: authorize TikTok APIs and create drafts only.

We do not sell personal data. Tokens are stored on the device running QW118 for this tool. Videos go to TikTok under TikTok’s privacy rules. You can revoke access in TikTok settings anytime.

For questions, use the email on your TikTok for Developers account.
