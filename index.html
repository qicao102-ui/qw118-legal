Desktop content tool

Upload finished videos to TikTok drafts.

QW118 is a desktop content tool for brands and creators. Authorize a TikTok account, upload a finished video, and QW118 places it in that account’s TikTok drafts (inbox). The creator reviews the draft and publishes it in the TikTok app. Nothing is posted automatically.

How it works

  1. Authorize TikTok

    Connect the TikTok account that should receive the video. QW118 uses that authorization to send the upload to the right creator.

  2. Upload a finished video

    Choose a completed video on your desktop. QW118 sends it to TikTok as a draft in the creator’s inbox, ready for review.

  3. Publish in the TikTok app

    Open TikTok, check the draft, and publish when it looks right. QW118 does not post, schedule, or publish for you.

For brands and creators

Use QW118 when the video is already finished and the last step is a person reviewing it inside TikTok. Campaign edits, client cuts, and creator videos can be handed off as drafts instead of going live from the desktop.

  • Brands can deliver an approved file into a creator’s TikTok inbox.
  • Creators keep the final post in the TikTok app.
  • A draft waits until someone chooses to publish it.

Drafts only. No auto-publish.

An upload from QW118 is a TikTok draft. It is not a live post. The creator decides if and when to publish.

Common questions

Does QW118 publish to a TikTok profile?

No. Videos are uploaded as drafts (inbox). Publishing happens later, in the TikTok app, after the creator reviews the draft.

Which account receives the video?

The TikTok account authorized in QW118. The upload is sent to that account’s drafts.
